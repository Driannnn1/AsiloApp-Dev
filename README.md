### Detective Conan
![Detective Conan Poster](https://github.com/Driannnn1/AsiloApp-Dev/blob/main/Detective%20Conan%20Poster.jpg?raw=true)
**Genre:** [Mystery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mystery_fiction), [Thriller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thriller_(genre))

**Case Closed**, also officially known as **Detective Conan** ([Japanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_language): 名探偵コナン, Hepburn: Meitantei Konan; lit. 'Great Detective Conan'), is a Japanese [manga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manga) series written and illustrated by Gosho Aoyama. It has been serialized in Shogakukan's *shōnen* manga magazine Weekly Shōnen Sunday since January 1994; its chapters have been collected in 109 tankōbon volumes as of October 2026. Because of legal problems with the name Detective Conan, several American English language releases were renamed Case Closed. The story follows the high school detective [Shinichi Kudo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinichi_Kudo), whose body was transformed into that of an elementary school-age child while investigating a mysterious organization. Generally, he solves a multitude of cases by impersonating his childhood best friend's father and various other characters.

### Death Note
![Death Note Poster](https://github.com/Driannnn1/AsiloApp-Dev/blob/main/Death%20Note.jpg?raw=true)

**Genre:** 	[Mystery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mystery_fiction), [Psychological thriller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological_thriller), [Supernatural thriller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supernatural_thriller)
**Death Note** (stylized in [all caps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_caps) is a Japanese [manga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manga) series written by [Tsugumi Ohba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsugumi_Ohba) and illustrated by [Takeshi Obata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Takeshi_Obata). It was serialized in Shueisha's shōnen manga magazine *Weekly Shōnen Jump* from December 2003 to May 2006, with its chapters collected in 12 tankōbon volumes. The story follows [Light Yagami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light_Yagami), a genius high school student who discovers a mysterious notebook, the Death Note. This notebook belonged to the shinigami Ryuk, and writing someone’s name in the pages leads to their death. The series centers on Light's use of the Death Note to carry out a worldwide massacre of individuals he deems immoral in an attempt to create a crime-free society, using the alias of a god-like vigilante named Kira. This leads to the efforts of an elite Japanese police task force, led by the enigmatic detective [L](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L_(Death_Note)), to apprehend him.

### Hajime No Ippo
![Hajme No Ippo Poster](https://github.com/Driannnn1/AsiloApp-Dev/blob/main/Hajime%20No%20Ippo%20Poster.jpg?raw=true)

**Genre:** [Action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action_fiction), [Comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comedy), [Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_manga)

***Hajime no Ippo*** (はじめの一歩; lit. 'The First Step') is a Japanese [boxing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boxing)-themed [manga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manga) series written and illustrated by [George Morikawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Morikawa). It has been serialized in Kodansha's [*shōnen manga magazine*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%8Dnen_manga) Weekly Shōnen Magazine since October 1989, with its chapters collected in 145 tankōbon volumes as of January 2026. It follows the story of high school student Ippo Makunouchi, as he begins his career in boxing and over time obtains many titles and defeats various opponents.

### Pokémon
![Pokemon Poster](https://github.com/Driannnn1/AsiloApp-Dev/blob/main/Pokemon%20Poster.jpg)

**Genre:** [Comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comedy), [Fantasy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fantasy_comedyhttps://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fantasy_comedy)

The anime franchise consists of eight sequential series in Japan, each based on a main installment of the [Pokémon video game series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_(video_game_series)). In the international broadcasts, these series are split across 29 seasons. The show originally followed [Ash Ketchum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ash_Ketchum), a young trainer of fictional creatures called [Pokémon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Pok%C3%A9mon). Joined by his partner Pokémon Pikachu and a rotating cast of human characters, Ash goes on a journey to become a "Pokémon Master", traveling through the various regions of the Pokémon world and competing in various Pokémon-battling tournaments known as the Pokémon League. Starting with the 26th season, a new cast is featured, centering on protagonists Liko and Roy.

### Dragon Ball
![Dragon Ball Poster](https://github.com/Driannnn1/AsiloApp-Dev/blob/main/Dragon%20Ball%20Poster.jpg)

**Genre:** [Comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comedy), [Fantasy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fantasy_comedyhttps://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fantasy_comedy)

**Dragon Ball** (([Japanese:](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_language) ドラゴンボール, Hepburn: Doragon Bōru) is a Japanese [media franchise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Media_franchise) created by [Akira Toriyama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akira_Toriyama). The series follows the adventures of protagonist [Son Goku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goku) from his childhood through adulthood as he trains in martial arts. Along his journey, he makes several friends, becomes a family man, discovers his alien heritage, and battles a wide variety of villains, many of whom, like him, seek the mystical orbs called the Dragon Balls in order to summon a wish-granting dragon.
