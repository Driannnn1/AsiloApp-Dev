### Detective Conan
![Detective Conan Poster](https://github.com/Driannnn1/AsiloApp-Dev/blob/main/Detective%20Conan%20Poster.jpg?raw=true)
**Genre:** [Mystery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mystery_fiction), [Thriller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thriller_(genre))

**Case Closed**, also officially known as **Detective Conan** ([Japanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_language): 名探偵コナン, Hepburn: Meitantei Konan; lit. 'Great Detective Conan'), is a Japanese [manga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manga) series written and illustrated by Gosho Aoyama. It has been serialized in Shogakukan's *shōnen* manga magazine Weekly Shōnen Sunday since January 1994; its chapters have been collected in 109 tankōbon volumes as of October 2026. Because of legal problems with the name Detective Conan, several American English language releases were renamed Case Closed. The story follows the high school detective [Shinichi Kudo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinichi_Kudo), whose body was transformed into that of an elementary school-age child while investigating a mysterious organization. Generally, he solves a multitude of cases by impersonating his childhood best friend's father and various other characters.

### Death Note
![Death Note Poster](https://github.com/Driannnn1/AsiloApp-Dev/blob/main/Death%20Note.jpg?raw=true)

**Genre:** 	[Mystery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mystery_fiction), [Psychological thriller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological_thriller), [Supernatural thriller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supernatural_thriller)
**Death Note** (stylized in [all caps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_caps) is a Japanese [manga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manga) series written by [Tsugumi Ohba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsugumi_Ohba) and illustrated by [Takeshi Obata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Takeshi_Obata). It was serialized in Shueisha's shōnen manga magazine *Weekly Shōnen Jump* from December 2003 to May 2006, with its chapters collected in 12 tankōbon volumes. The story follows [Light Yagami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light_Yagami), a genius high school student who discovers a mysterious notebook, the Death Note. This notebook belonged to the shinigami Ryuk, and writing someone’s name in the pages leads to their death. The series centers on Light's use of the Death Note to carry out a worldwide massacre of individuals he deems immoral in an attempt to create a crime-free society, using the alias of a god-like vigilante named Kira. This leads to the efforts of an elite Japanese police task force, led by the enigmatic detective [L](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L_(Death_Note)), to apprehend him.
